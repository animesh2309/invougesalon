About Us

A Luxurious Escape
in Benachity

Located on the 2nd floor of Raj Sammilonee MAX Mall in Durgapur, Invogue Salon & Spa provides an unparalleled beauty experience. From high-end hair styling to relaxing body massages and intricate nail art, our expert team brings out your absolute best.

We are proud to be a women-owned, fully accessible, and LGBTQ+ friendly establishment. With over 227 glowing reviews, our commitment is to provide a clean, welcoming, and purely professional environment for everyone.
